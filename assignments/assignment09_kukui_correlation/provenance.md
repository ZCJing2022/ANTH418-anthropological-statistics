# Data provenance

The teaching CSV retains all 71 site records from the published S1 workbook. It includes sampling coordinates, elevation, annual rainfall, and site summaries of kukui δ¹³C and δ¹⁵N. `source_row_id` records the order of the data rows in the supplement. Sites were sampled on Hawaiʻi Island. Isotope measurements summarize multiple fruits per site. [Lincoln, McCoy and Ladefoged (2018)](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0204654) · [Published S1 table](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0204654.s001)

## Coordinate limitation

Source rows 61, 62 and 66 have longitudes of −155878799, −155879399 and −155879906, respectively. These are outside the valid longitude range in degrees. The values are preserved as published. Verify them before using those coordinates for mapping, distance calculations or other spatial analysis. The assigned correlations use elevation, rainfall and δ¹³C, so all 71 sites remain in the exercise.
