# Codebook

Teaching file: `knipper2020_humans.csv`

**Unit of analysis:** one human individual represented in the teaching dataset.

| Variable | Type | Meaning | Units |
|---|---|---|---|
| `source_row_id` | integer | Source-row identifier preserved during teaching-file construction | — |
| `site` | categorical | Archaeological site (La Bastida or Gatas) | — |
| `d13c` | numeric | Human bone-collagen carbon isotope value | ‰ |
| `d15n` | numeric | Human bone-collagen nitrogen isotope value | ‰ |
| `sex` | categorical | Recorded osteological sex, when available | — |
| `age_category` | categorical | Recorded age category, when available | — |

> This codebook describes the standardized teaching file, not every variable in the original published dataset.
