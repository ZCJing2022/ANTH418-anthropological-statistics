# Assignment 1: Bronze Age Diet and Farming at La Bastida and Gatas

**Week:** 4<br>
**Statistical focus:** Measures of Central Tendency and Dispersion

## Research question

How should selected human stable-isotope distributions at La Bastida and Gatas be described, and what do different measures of center and dispersion reveal?

## Published case study

Knipper, Corina, Cristina Rihuete-Herrada, Jordi Voltas, Petra Held, Vicente Lull, Rafael Micó, Roberto Risch, and Kurt W. Alt. 2020. Reconstructing Bronze Age Diets and Farming Strategies at the Early Bronze Age Sites of La Bastida and Gatas (Southeast Iberia) Using Stable Isotope Analysis. PLOS ONE 15(3): e0229398.

DOI: https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0229398

## Workflow

1. Read the case-study article before analysis.
2. Read `codebook.md` and `provenance.md`.
3. Open `assignment01_knipper_descriptive.qmd`.
4. Render once before editing to confirm that R, packages, and data paths work.
5. Work through **Code → Describe → Visualize → Analyze → Visualize the result → Interpret**.
6. Keep only output needed to support your reasoning.

## Recurring interpretation questions

1. What is the unit of analysis?
2. What statistical result matters most?
3. What archaeological or anthropological claim does the result support?
4. What claim does the result not support?
